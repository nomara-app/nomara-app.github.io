How do I track my measurements?

Open Nomara and add a measurement entry. You can record your weight and body measurements, then review previous entries in your measurement history.

Can I edit a measurement?

Yes. Open your measurement history, find the entry you want to update and tap the edit icon.

Where are my photos and measurements stored?

Your progress photos and measurements are stored locally on your device. Nomara does not currently offer account-based cloud synchronization or recovery. Removing the app may permanently delete locally stored data.

What is Nomara Full?

Nomara Full is an optional subscription that unlocks progress charts, long-term measurement insights and photo comparison tools.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Nomara, go to the Nomara Full subscription screen and select Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with your subscription.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open your iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Nomara Full if it appears there. You can also manage subscriptions here. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Can I get a refund?

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund through Apple's Report a Problem website.
