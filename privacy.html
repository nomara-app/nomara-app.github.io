Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Nomara is designed to keep your personal body measurements and progress photos on your device. You do not need to create an account to use the app. Nomara is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

1. About Nomara

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Nomara: Body Measurements ("Nomara"). For privacy questions, contact support.nomara@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter

Nomara allows you to record body measurements, weight, progress photos, measurement dates and app preferences. This information is stored locally on your device. Nomara does not require an account or upload your measurement history and progress photos to a Nomara-operated server.

3. Photos and permissions

If you choose to add progress photos, Nomara uses the photos you select for your personal photo journal and comparison features. Photo access is used for the functionality you request.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Nomara offers an optional paid subscription called Nomara Full. Purchases and subscription billing are handled through Apple's App Store. Nomara uses RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements and restore purchases.

Apple and RevenueCat may process information related to your purchases, subscription status, transaction identifiers and technical identifiers needed to provide subscription functionality. Nomara does not receive your full payment card details.

You can review their privacy practices here: Apple Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

If you enable measurement reminders, Nomara may schedule local notifications on your device. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

6. Data storage and deletion

Your body measurements and progress photos are stored locally on your device. You can manage your entries within the app. Uninstalling Nomara may permanently remove locally stored app data. Nomara does not currently provide account-based synchronization or cloud recovery.

7. Data sharing

Nomara does not sell your body measurements or progress photos. Subscription-related information is processed by Apple and RevenueCat as described above. Information may also be processed by operating-system services needed for app functionality.

8. Data security

Nomara is designed to minimize the collection of personal information by keeping your measurements and photos on your device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so you should also protect access to your device.

9. Age restriction

Nomara is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 or older. The app is not intended for use by children or anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided personal information in connection with Nomara, please contact us at support.nomara@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For questions about privacy or data handling, contact: support.nomara@gmail.com.
