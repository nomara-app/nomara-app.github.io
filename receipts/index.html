Nomara: Receipts

Keep the purchase.
Keep the details.

A local place for your purchases, receipts and attachments. Find the details you need, review estimated complaint deadlines and keep copies of your records.

Records, together

Enter and edit purchases, search your records and keep original JPG/JPEG, PNG or PDF receipts with them. Each attachment can be up to 5 MiB.

Less typing with Plus

On supported iOS and Android devices, on-device OCR helps turn receipt images into purchase details. Review the results before saving. PDF text extraction is not supported.

Copies you control

Export CSV files, purchase/receipt ZIP archives or original receipts. Plus adds versioned backups and validated restore with merge or explicitly confirmed replace.

A few details worth keeping.

Purchase and receipt records are stored locally. Receipt data has no application-level encryption, and backup and export files are unencrypted. Protect your device and saved copies. Apple and RevenueCat process billing information.

Plus access, including Lifetime, requires periodic verification; verified access lasts up to three days offline, or an earlier applicable subscription expiry. Archive safety limits apply, including 100 MiB per ZIP. Estimated complaint deadlines are guidance to check independently, not a determination of your legal rights.

Receipts is for a general audience, including users under 18. Minors may need parental or guardian permission for paid purchases.
