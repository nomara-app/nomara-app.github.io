Nomara: Receipts

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Your purchases, receipts and attachments are stored locally on your device. Apple and RevenueCat process information for in-app purchases. Backups, exports and support requests can also involve services outside your device.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Nomara: Receipts. Marlene Bech-Andersen is the individual publisher and data controller for Nomara: Receipts, operating under the Nomara brand, and also publishes Nomara: Body Measurements. In this policy, “we” and “us” refer to Marlene Bech-Andersen. This policy covers Receipts; Body Measurements has its own privacy policy. For privacy questions, email support.nomara@gmail.com.

2. Local records and attachments

The purchase information you enter, receipts you add and associated attachments are stored locally on your device. Native apps use a local Sembast database; the web app uses Sembast Web in browser storage. Receipt data is not encrypted at application level. Documents may contain personal information such as names, addresses, purchased items and payment references. Only include information you need and have the right to store.

When you select a photo or file, or capture a receipt with the camera, the selected content is used for the action you request. Manage camera and photo permissions in your device settings. Receipts support JPG/JPEG, PNG and PDF attachments up to 5 MiB (5,242,880 bytes) per file. Original files may retain embedded metadata, such as image location information.

3. Optional text recognition (OCR) and reminders

Plus offers optional on-device OCR using Apple Vision on iOS and bundled Tesseract models on Android. The app does not send your receipt images to a cloud OCR service. OCR extracts text from supported receipt images, not PDFs, to help you enter purchase information. Check extracted text and correct mistakes before relying on it.

Plus also offers local deadline reminders. Manage notification permissions in your device settings. Backups can include reminder preferences, but not device permissions. Local notifications may display product names on the lock screen.

4. Backups and exports

Free includes CSV export, purchase/receipt ZIP export and original receipt export. Plus adds backup creation and restore functionality. Versioned ZIP backups contain purchases and original receipts and can include reminder preferences. Billing entitlements and device permissions are excluded. The updated app restores supported version-1 and version-2 Nomara Receipts backups. Version-1 records use DKK; version-2 backups preserve each purchase currency. Ordinary purchase/receipt ZIP exports, CSV files and unrelated ZIP files cannot be restored through this feature. New version-2 backups require an app version that supports that format.

Restore validates archive structure and integrity before showing a preview, rejects previews if local data has changed, and applies changes in a database transaction. Merge preserves existing purchases with conflicting IDs and skips duplicates or receipt-ID conflicts. The Replace option replaces the entire purchase and receipt collection only after explicit confirmation. Applying reminder preferences is optional and off by default.

Backup and export files are not encrypted. Store them securely and share them only with intended recipients. Original receipt exports retain the original file contents and may include personal metadata.

If you save a file to cloud storage or share it with another app or person, that destination receives it and handles it under its own privacy practices. Device backup settings may also affect whether app data is included in a device or cloud backup. Manage exported files and backups separately from the app.

Purchase currency is stored with the amount. Selecting a currency does not convert the amount. Pending payment status is stored separately on the device to help prevent repeated purchases; this status is not included in receipt backups.

5. Apple purchases

For App Store purchases, Apple processes payments for the Plus monthly subscription and Lifetime purchase. Apple handles payment details and purchase records under its own terms and privacy policy. Nomara does not receive your full payment card details from Apple. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. RevenueCat purchase management

Nomara uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, manage Plus access and restore purchases. Apple and RevenueCat may process information related to your purchases, subscription status, transaction identifiers and technical identifiers needed to provide purchase and subscription functionality. The app uses an anonymous RevenueCat customer identifier and does not supply a custom user ID, email, subscriber attributes or stored shopping receipt contents to the billing adapter. Automatic device-identifier collection and SDK diagnostics are disabled in the app configuration; this does not eliminate billing-related device or network processing. An App Store purchase receipt is a billing record for an app purchase; it is distinct from the shopping receipts you store in Nomara.

Purchase management involves third-party processing outside your device. You can review RevenueCat's privacy practices in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. This link does not replace Nomara's disclosures in this policy. Contact Nomara support for privacy requests relating to this processing.

Where the GDPR applies, we process the purchase identifiers and entitlement information necessary to provide paid access and restore eligible purchases on the basis of performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)). Without this information, we cannot verify or restore paid access. This basis covers necessary purchase management; it does not extend to purchase analytics for service improvement.

RevenueCat also processes purchase history for dashboard analytics, including customer history and charts, as described in its purchase-data disclosure guidance. These records concern in-app purchases.

RevenueCat provides information about its processing terms, subprocessors and international data-transfer safeguards in its current Data Processing Addendum and Terms of Use. Its Privacy Policy explains its privacy practices and distinguishes processing on behalf of app providers from processing for its own purposes. These references do not replace this policy or your right to contact Nomara about your information.

No additional integrations are enabled in Nomara Receipts' RevenueCat project.

7. Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send and use them to respond to your request. Email providers, including the provider of our Gmail support mailbox, process this correspondence. See Google's Privacy Policy. Avoid sending full payment details or unnecessary personal information.

Where responding to your support request is necessary to perform our contract with you, or to take steps you request before entering into a contract, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other ordinary enquiries and follow-up correspondence, the basis is our legitimate interest in answering questions and resolving reported app problems (Article 6(1)(f)), only where necessary and where your rights and interests do not override that interest. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting support.nomara@gmail.com. Sending an email is not treated as consent to unrelated processing.

We use Gmail for support. Google processes email content and attachments under its Privacy Policy. Google describes global processing, which may include processing outside the EEA. Its transfer frameworks describe adequacy decisions, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers to certified Google entities, and Standard Contractual Clauses where required for transfers not covered by adequacy. That page explains how to request copies of the clauses from Google.

8. Retention, deletion and security

Ordinary support emails and their attachments will generally be deleted no later than 12 months after the support case is closed. We delete this information earlier when it is no longer needed, unless longer retention is necessary for a documented lawful purpose or legal obligation. This support-data retention policy does not set the retention period for Apple or RevenueCat billing records.

Apple holds payment information and App Store purchase records under its own policies. Its App Store privacy notice explains that purchase and download records are retained for financial-reporting requirements that vary by region and to enable re-downloads. These Apple retention rules do not set a retention period for RevenueCat.

RevenueCat processes in-app purchase history and entitlement records to support purchase management. The 12-month support-email rule does not apply to these records. For information about RevenueCat's current data-handling terms, see its Data Processing Addendum; its customer documentation describes available customer-deletion functionality. These references are not a promise of a particular deletion deadline or immediate erasure of every copy. To request access to or deletion of information processed on Nomara's behalf, contact support.nomara@gmail.com. Deleting local app data or requesting deletion of billing information does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage cancellation separately through Apple.

Local records remain in app storage until removed or the storage is lost. Clearing browser data or browser storage eviction may remove web-app records. Removing the app or losing your device may cause loss of local data. Keep a current backup before deleting or reinstalling the app. Removing local records does not delete exported files, cloud backups, support emails or records held by Apple and RevenueCat. Apple retains its records under its own policies and applicable obligations. Deleting local app data does not itself request deletion of support correspondence or information processed on Nomara's behalf. Contact us at the support address below for help with those records.

Deletion is not a promise of secure erasure: copies may remain in database journals, operating-system backups or temporary export/share caches. Protect your device and backup locations against unauthorized access. No electronic storage or transfer method is completely secure. Contact support about information held in connection with a support request or purchase management.

9. Your privacy choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You may also have a right to complain to your local data protection authority. Send privacy requests to support.nomara@gmail.com.

10. Adults only and children's privacy

Nomara: Receipts is intended for adults aged 18 and older (18+). It is not intended for children under 18.

Local receipt storage does not mean that billing or support information stays on the device. The 18+ minimum age does not by itself establish compliance with children's privacy requirements worldwide. Children's privacy requirements vary by jurisdiction and may require additional notices, parental consent or other safeguards for particular processing.

If you believe that information about someone under 18 has been provided to Nomara or processed on its behalf, contact support.nomara@gmail.com. Users, parents and guardians can use this address to raise privacy concerns or request review or deletion of information handled by Nomara, subject to applicable law. Please describe the concern without initially sending full receipts, identity documents or unnecessary information about the child.
