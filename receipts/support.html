Nomara: Receipts

Support

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Help with Nomara: Receipts, your purchase records, receipts and attachments.

Contact support

Nomara: Receipts is published by Marlene Bech-Andersen under the Nomara brand, who also publishes Nomara: Body Measurements. Both apps use the same support contact.

Email support.nomara@gmail.com. Include “Nomara: Receipts”, your app version, device model, iOS or Android version and a description of the issue. Steps to reproduce it and a redacted screenshot can help. Never send Apple Account passwords or full payment card details.

What is the minimum age for Nomara: Receipts?

Nomara: Receipts is intended for adults aged 18 and older (18+). It is not intended for children under 18.

If you believe that information about someone under 18 has been provided to Nomara or processed on its behalf, email support.nomara@gmail.com. Users, parents and guardians can contact us about a child's privacy or an unexpected purchase. Avoid sending full receipts, passwords or identity documents with the initial request. See the Privacy Policy for information handling and privacy requests.

What can I do with the app?

Keep purchase information together with receipts and attachments. Optional OCR extracts text to help you enter information. Backup/export tools let you keep copies of records. Check the app's Free and Plus descriptions for feature availability and limits.

What are Free, Plus and Lifetime?

Free includes up to 10 purchases, one receipt per purchase, manual entry and editing, search, viewing, estimated complaint deadlines, CSV export, purchase/receipt ZIP export and original receipt export.

Plus includes unlimited purchases, up to five receipts per purchase, on-device OCR, local deadline reminders and versioned ZIP backups with restore functionality, in addition to the Free features.

The App Store displays the local price and currency for the monthly auto-renewing subscription and Lifetime one-time purchase before you confirm payment. Read the Terms of Use.

OCR results are missing or incorrect

OCR requires Plus and processes images on-device using Apple Vision on iOS and bundled Tesseract models on Android. It recognizes text in supported images; it does not extract text from PDF attachments.

Try a clear, well-lit image of the full receipt without glare or blur. Check extracted text against the original and correct mistakes before relying on it. OCR may misread dates, amounts and merchant names. If recognition fails, enter the information manually and contact support with a redacted example if needed.

I cannot add a receipt or attachment

Use JPG/JPEG, PNG or PDF files up to 5 MiB (5,242,880 bytes) each. Free allows one receipt per purchase; Plus allows up to five. Check whether you have reached that limit, whether device storage is available and whether the file is accessible. Review camera or photo permissions in your device settings. Files stored with a cloud provider may need to download first. If the issue continues, tell support the file type, approximate size and any error message, without sending private document contents initially.

How do I restore an Apple purchase of Plus or Lifetime?

  1. Use the Apple Account associated with the original App Store purchase.
  2. Connect to the internet and open Nomara: Receipts.
  3. Use Restore Purchases in the app and allow verification to complete.

If access is still missing, check whether your subscription is active or your Lifetime purchase appears in Apple's purchase history. Restart the app and contact support if needed. Do not buy again just to troubleshoot. Restoration recovers eligible paid access, not stored shopping receipts or attachments.

How do I cancel or request a refund for an Apple purchase?

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select Nomara: Receipts, then choose Cancel Subscription, or manage subscriptions with Apple. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current billing period ends to avoid the next renewal. Cancellation normally leaves Plus access available until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription. Lifetime has no recurring charge to cancel. Submit refund requests through Apple's Report a Problem service.

How do exports and backups differ?

Free includes CSV export, purchase/receipt ZIP export and original receipt export. Versioned ZIP backups contain purchases and original receipts and can include reminder preferences. Billing entitlements and device permissions are excluded. The updated app restores supported version-1 and version-2 Nomara Receipts backups. Version-1 records use DKK; version-2 backups preserve each purchase currency. Ordinary purchase/receipt ZIP exports, CSV files and unrelated ZIP files cannot be restored through this feature. New version-2 backups require an app version that supports that format.

Choose a location you can access later and check that the file was saved successfully. Backup and export files are not encrypted. Store them securely and share them only with intended recipients. A purchase/receipt ZIP export preserves readable records and original attachments, but is not a restorable backup and does not include reminder preferences. ZIP creation and restore have safety limits of 100 MiB per archive, 10,000 purchases and 5,000 receipts, including an 8 MiB manifest limit. These limits apply even with Plus; export original receipts separately if a collection exceeds them.

How do I save a backup?

With Plus, tap Create Backup in Settings > Backup & Export. Wait for generation, then choose a location in the iOS save dialog. The app confirms saving after the dialog completes successfully. Cancelling is not a completed backup; you can try again. There is no separate Save file step.

How do I restore a backup?

  1. Keep the original backup unchanged and save a current backup before restoring.
  2. Tap Restore to open the file picker and select a supported version-1 or version-2 Nomara Receipts backup. Ordinary ZIP exports and CSV files are not accepted.
  3. Review the preview after archive structure and integrity validation. A preview becomes invalid if your local data changes; generate a new preview before continuing. If validation fails, preserve the file and contact support with the error message.
  4. Choose merge or replace. Merge adds nonconflicting purchases, preserves existing conflicting purchases and skips duplicates or receipt-ID conflicts. Replace requires explicit confirmation to replace the entire purchase and receipt collection. Changes are applied transactionally.

Reminder preferences are applied only if you select that option and the archive includes them; the option is off by default. Restoring a backup does not restore paid access or grant device permissions. Use purchase restoration separately and check notification permissions for reminders. For help, send support the app version that created the backup and any error details; avoid sending the full backup initially.

What happens when Plus access ends?

Your saved purchases, receipts and backup files are kept. Existing over-limit records remain viewable, editable and exportable. Free blocks new purchases when 10 or more are already saved and prevents increasing receipt counts above one per purchase. You can still replace or remove existing receipts, including those retained after a downgrade. Backup creation, archive restore and OCR require Plus. Reminders are cancelled when the app reconciles access; preferences remain. Already scheduled notifications may persist until the app runs again.

Why does Lifetime need an internet connection?

Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Previously verified Apple Lifetime access remains available offline and is revalidated when connectivity returns. Verified refunds or revocations can remove access. Reconnect and refresh if Plus becomes unavailable. The current paywall does not allow a new Lifetime purchase while Monthly Plus is active. Apple purchases are not automatically transferred to Google Play.

How do complaint deadlines and reminders work?

Free shows estimated complaint deadlines. Verify the deadline that applies to your purchase independently. Plus adds local deadline reminders. If reminders are missing, check reminder preferences and device notification permissions. Backups include preferences but not permissions; check these after restoring. Do not rely solely on reminders to meet a deadline.

The app is not working or records are missing

Close and reopen the app, check available storage and look for an app update. If you have Plus, create a versioned backup before reinstalling. With Free, export a purchase/receipt ZIP to preserve readable copies of records and receipts; this export cannot be restored through the backup feature. CSV and original-receipt exports also preserve copies but cannot be imported through the archive restore feature. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step: local records may be lost. If data is missing, preserve any backups and contact support before making further changes. Recovery depends on the copies available.

Where is my information stored?

Purchase records, shopping receipts and attachments are stored locally in a Sembast database on native devices or Sembast Web in the browser. Receipt data is not encrypted at application level. Apple and RevenueCat process information for paid access. Backups, exports and support emails may involve other providers. Read the Privacy Policy.
