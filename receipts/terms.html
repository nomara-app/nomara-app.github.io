2. Free and Plus features

Free includes up to 10 purchases, one receipt per purchase, manual entry and editing, search, viewing, estimated complaint deadlines, CSV export, purchase/receipt ZIP export and original receipt export.

Plus includes unlimited purchases, up to five receipts per purchase, on-device OCR, local deadline reminders and versioned ZIP backups with restore functionality, in addition to the Free features.

Receipts support JPG/JPEG, PNG and PDF attachments up to 5 MiB (5,242,880 bytes) per file. OCR uses Apple Vision on iOS and bundled Tesseract models on Android to process images on-device. It supports images, not PDF text extraction.

6. Cancellation

Open iPhone or iPad Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select Nomara: Receipts, then choose Cancel Subscription. If Apple shows an expiry date without a cancellation option, the subscription may already be cancelled. You can also manage subscriptions with Apple. Cancellation normally leaves access available until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. See Apple's cancellation instructions.

When Plus access ends, existing purchases and receipts remain available, including records above Free limits. You can continue viewing, editing and exporting existing data. New purchases are blocked while you have 10 or more, and you cannot increase receipt counts beyond the Free limit. Replacing or removing existing receipts remains available. Backup creation, archive restore, OCR and local reminders require Plus. Previously saved backup files are not deleted; reminder preferences are retained.

7. Restoring purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the app with an internet connection and the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoration verifies eligible purchases, including an active monthly subscription or Lifetime purchase. Restoring an expired subscription does not reactivate paid access. RevenueCat helps manage purchase access. Restoring purchases does not recover locally stored shopping receipts, attachments or purchase records.

9. Your responsibilities

Use the app in accordance with applicable laws and only store or share content you have the right to use. Do not misuse, interfere with or attempt to compromise the app or its services. Check manually entered information and OCR results. Complaint deadlines are estimates, not a determination of your legal rights. Verify relevant deadlines independently and do not rely solely on reminders. Keep original documents where needed and maintain backups. Do not rely solely on the app to satisfy tax, accounting, warranty or other recordkeeping requirements.

Secure your device and choose appropriate destinations for exported files. Removing the app or losing your device may result in loss of local records. Purchase restoration is separate from data recovery.

Versioned ZIP backups contain purchases and original receipts and can include reminder preferences. Billing entitlements and device permissions are excluded. The updated app restores supported version-1 and version-2 Nomara Receipts backups. Version-1 records use DKK; version-2 backups preserve each purchase currency. Ordinary purchase/receipt ZIP exports, CSV files and unrelated ZIP files cannot be restored through this feature. New version-2 backups require an app version that supports that format.

Restore validates archive structure and integrity before showing a preview, rejects previews if local data has changed, and applies changes in a database transaction. Merge preserves existing purchases with conflicting IDs and skips duplicates or receipt-ID conflicts. The Replace option replaces the entire purchase and receipt collection only after explicit confirmation. Applying reminder preferences is optional and off by default.

ZIP creation and restore have safety limits of 100 MiB per archive, 10,000 purchases and 5,000 receipts, including an 8 MiB manifest limit. These limits apply even with Plus; export original receipts separately if a collection exceeds them. Review the preview carefully and preserve a current backup before replacing data. Receipt data is not encrypted at application level. Backup and export files are not encrypted. Store them securely and share them only with intended recipients.
